        Using two screens or a phone

        Two screens: extend your desktop. Press V in the slides to open this window, then keep these notes on your laptop and the slides on the projector. In Follow mode, these buttons advance the audience slide—including its reveals.

        Browse independently: look ahead without moving the audience. Follow audience returns to its current position.

        Phone: transfer notes.html and open it in a browser that runs local HTML. Swipe left/right; scroll longer cards vertically. Phone navigation is manual, not automatically linked to your laptop. No connection is needed for the note text.

        Keys: arrows/space for next and previous; Home for first. Text buttons adjust cue size. Sources stay collapsed while you speak.

        Important: N in the audience deck opens notes on that screen. Use V for a separate presenter window. Keep displays extended, not mirrored.
