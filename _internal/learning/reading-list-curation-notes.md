# Reading List Curation Notes

Private editorial notes for maintaining `content/reading/index.md`.

- Keep the public reading page honest: a curated personal list, not a performative bibliography.
- Add new material only after it has become a real influence or a resource still worth recommending after the novelty has worn off.
- Avoid turning the page into a generic syllabus or a trend-tracking list.
- Prefer durable references, recurring influences, and resources that connect to the site's engineering point of view.
