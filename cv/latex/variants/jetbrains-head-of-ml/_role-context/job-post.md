## Job Description

JetBrains describes the role as part of its move beyond standalone developer tools toward a unified, AI-native platform for software development.

AI is framed as becoming an active participant in how software is planned, built, reviewed, and operated across teams and organisations. The new challenges are not solved at individual-tool level alone: governance, security, cost control, observability, and coordinated work between humans and autonomous agents.

JetBrains' goal is to build a platform that enables companies to adopt AI in software development in a structured, scalable, and economically efficient manner without locking them into closed ecosystems. The platform will serve as the execution and governance layer for AI-driven development, integrated with developer tools but designed to work across teams, products, and environments.

JetBrains is looking for an experienced ML leader who has created products with an ML backbone, weaving together research, technical excellence, and strong product focus.

The role requires strength across:

- technology;
- product vision;
- business operations;
- cooperation with products across the company, including AI-native products and products integrating AI.

## In This Role, You Will

- Identify, prioritize, and champion high-impact AI projects that align with JetBrains' product strategy and deliver measurable business value.
- Lead a team of about 50 people across multiple AI initiatives, including hiring and developing team leads, ML engineers, and MLOps specialists.
- Set the technical direction for JetBrains' ML roadmap, translating cutting-edge AI advancements into practical product innovations.
- Ensure projects deliver on commitments and create measurable value.
- Set technical standards, architectural direction, and best practices for ML development.

## Requirements

- Ability to balance long-term R&D with short-term product needs.
- Track record of shipping production ML systems that users actually use.
- Experience building business cases for technical projects.
- Deep understanding of modern ML infrastructure, LLMs, and AI tooling.
- Outstanding stakeholder management skills and comfort working with senior leaders.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Excellent management and communication skills.
- Attention to detail.

## Especially Valued

- Distributed training of multi-billion parameter models.
- CI systems, workflow automation, and experiment tracking.
- Kotlin programming language.
