## Job Description Summary

Spotify's Data Platform studio owns two critical pillars:

- an event delivery platform collecting trillions of events per day from producers to consumers;
- a pseudonymisation platform that enables responsible, privacy-compliant data use.

These systems are central to Spotify's data collection and are used by hundreds of teams across the company.

Spotify wants an Engineering Manager to lead a team of six experienced engineers distributed across Europe. The manager will set technical direction, ensure delivery of high-impact work, and invest deeply in engineers' growth and careers.

## What The Role Does

- Lead and develop a team of six experienced engineers, with focus on coaching, career development, and creating conditions for strong work.
- Take accountability for team delivery, clear priorities, healthy execution, and measurable outcomes.
- Partner with product and stakeholders to shape roadmap for event delivery and privacy infrastructure.
- Drive technical strategy and architectural decisions for systems operating at Spotify scale.
- Build a culture of ownership, psychological safety, and continuous improvement.
- Collaborate across Data Platform and wider engineering organisation to align shared goals and dependencies.
- Manage on-call health, operational excellence, and reliability for team services.
- Participate in hiring, onboarding, and growing team capabilities.
