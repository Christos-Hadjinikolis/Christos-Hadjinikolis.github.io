# Cover Letter

Dear Apple AiDP recruiting team,

I am applying for the Principal Machine Learning Engineer role because the problem space sits where my experience has been converging: turning advanced AI capabilities into reliable, observable, product-quality systems. My strongest background is not in isolated model experimentation, but in the engineering layer that determines whether intelligent systems can be trusted in production: inference services, model lifecycle discipline, distributed data flows, evaluation, monitoring, rollout safety, and maintainable platform architecture.

For the last several years at Vortexa, I have worked as a staff-level ML Tech Lead and Pod Lead, designing and delivering production ML systems for real-time maritime intelligence. This has included destination-prediction services, backend model-serving systems, Kafka/Flink-based data flows, CI/CD for model workflows, online evaluation, fallback paths, health checks, and traceable performance monitoring. Earlier, at Vodafone, I worked on an internal MLOps platform using Kubeflow on GCP, supporting model versioning, deployment, telemetry, and resource-governed experimentation. Across these roles, the consistent theme has been taking modelling work out of the lab and making it dependable enough for real users and operational decisions.

I am also actively building Promet, my reference project for applied LLM engineering. Promet is a local-first AI workbench for memory-aware, tool-using agents, with Ollama as the first supported runtime. The point of the project is to demonstrate the infrastructure around LLM adoption: runtime adapters, orchestration boundaries, prompt construction, streaming behaviour, tool execution, permissioning, local configuration, traceability, and deterministic verification loops. It reflects how I think about generative AI products: model quality matters, but so do the harness, interfaces, evaluation path, safety boundaries, and failure modes around the model.

I want to be direct about fit. I bring strong production ML systems experience, senior technical leadership, Python and Java engineering, distributed data infrastructure, and applied LLM systems architecture. I do not want to overstate experience I have not yet earned: I have not led deep RLHF/RLAIF programmes, large-scale LLM pre-training, or extensive LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning work. Those are areas I am deliberately growing into. What I would bring immediately is the discipline of a production ML engineer who understands that generative AI adoption succeeds when model customisation is paired with rigorous evaluation, robust serving, clear tool governance, privacy-conscious architecture, and systems that are inspectable when they fail.

That is why AiDP is compelling to me. Apple's bar for performance, privacy, reliability, and craft makes the platform layer of AI unusually important. I would be excited to contribute where my experience is strongest: bridging research and product, designing reliable inference and evaluation systems, mentoring engineers, and building the infrastructure that lets internal generative AI capabilities become useful, safe, and maintainable at scale. I would also welcome the opportunity to deepen my work in model customisation, alignment, and Apple-specific product integration within a team operating at that standard.
