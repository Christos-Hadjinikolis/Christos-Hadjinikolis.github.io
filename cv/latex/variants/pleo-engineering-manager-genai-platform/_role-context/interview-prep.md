# Interview Prep

## Five Strongest Interview Stories

1. **Kafka Streams to Flink architectural transition**
   - Story: led a two-year platform transition for critical live pipelines.
   - Angle for Pleo: architectural inflection point, technical trade-offs, maintaining delivery velocity, operational visibility, state/checkpointing, and Java/Python pragmatism.

2. **Building Vortexa's ML lifecycle from low MLOps maturity**
   - Story: introduced research/prototyping discipline, MLflow, model/data versioning, service-based model deployment, replay/evaluation interfaces, and live accuracy monitoring.
   - Angle for Pleo: directly relevant to trustworthy LLM/agentic platform outputs without claiming LLM-specific tooling ownership.

3. **Operating a client-facing ML/streaming estate with MTTR under 30 minutes**
   - Story: observability, runbooks, rollback/fallback plans, Jira alerts, and shared on-call ownership.
   - Angle for Pleo: production readiness, incident response, SRE partnership, and operational trust for internal platform customers.

4. **Scaling Data Production Team from 4 to 30+**
   - Story: shaped hiring loops, system-design interviews, onboarding, mentoring, ownership boundaries, and progression.
   - Angle for Pleo: hiring plan, technical-team development, retention, and creating the conditions for autonomous engineers to ship.

5. **Developer enablement through replay, local testing, and public tooling**
   - Story: local E2E/integration tests, Backstage adoption, dev containers, DynamicIO, skeleton-replay, and JetBrains plugin.
   - Angle for Pleo: platform team empathy, AI-assisted engineering, review throughput, evaluation evidence, and making complex systems understandable.
