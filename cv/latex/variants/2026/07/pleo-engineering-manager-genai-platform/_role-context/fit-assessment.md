## Tailoring Choices

- Repositioned the headline to `Engineering Manager | AI/ML Platforms & Streaming Systems`.
- Reframed Vortexa as a live ML and streaming platform estate rather than maritime prediction detail.
- Foregrounded platform roadmap, stakeholder alignment, architecture change, trustworthy ML lifecycle, production readiness, and engineering operating model.
- Kept team-growth and hiring evidence visible because Pleo emphasizes hiring and team development.
- Moved selected platform/tooling work to the top of page 2 to support developer enablement, evaluation, and AI-assisted workflow claims.
- Kept UCL/JTC'21 standards work close to the top of page 2 because Pleo cares about trustworthy AI outputs and governance.

## Claims Intentionally Not Made

- No claim of production LiteLLM ownership.
- No claim of MCP gateway or tool-registry ownership.
- No claim of BrainTrust, Temporal, or LLM gateway production experience.
- No claim of owning LLM spend or formal budget.
- No claim of being a GenAI infrastructure expert.
- No claim of managing multiple teams or a team of Staff/Senior engineers.
- No claim of Kubernetes expertise beyond keeping AWS/GCP and platform experience visible.

## Summary

The strongest fit argument is not "I already run a GenAI platform." It is:

> I have led production ML platform evolution at meaningful scale, including evaluation, deployment, observability, governance, reliability, developer enablement, hiring, and architectural change. Those are the same operating problems a GenAI platform team must solve as LLM infrastructure becomes product-critical.
