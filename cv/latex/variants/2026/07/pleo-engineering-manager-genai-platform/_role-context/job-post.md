## Role Summary

Pleo's GenAI Platform is an internal AI infrastructure team. It owns the platform that customer-facing AI features are built on, including LLM serving, MCP gateway/tool registry, agentic runtime, evaluation tooling, and observability for trustworthy AI outputs at scale.

The role is a technical engineering management role for a builder-leader. The manager leads staff/senior engineers through an architectural transition from Kotlin-first SDK to hybrid Kotlin/Python architecture for agentic development.

The role reports to the VP of Data and works with Product, Engineering leadership, Data, Security, Risk, and Legal.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead the GenAI Platform team day to day: delivery, quality, team development, and ways of working.
- Own execution of platform roadmap: LLM gateway, agentic runtime, MCP layer, data retrieval, evaluation tooling, hybrid architecture transition.
- Manage stakeholder relationships with Data & AI Products as primary internal customer.
- Drive hiring to build the team.
- Partner with SRE on reliability standards, incident management, and production readiness.
- Represent the team in leadership and cross-functional discussions.
- Own cost and resourcing decisions: LLM spend, infrastructure costs, headcount.
