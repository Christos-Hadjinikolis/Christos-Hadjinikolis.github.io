## Composite Requirements Used For Tailoring

The strongest common signal is not "pure people manager". These roles favour deeply technical managers who can still reason about architecture, production systems, and engineering quality.

Primary requirements:

- 8-12+ years in software engineering, ML systems, infrastructure, or data-intensive platforms.
- 2-5+ years in engineering management, technical leadership, or team-lead ownership.
- Experience leading engineers through ambiguous technical work and translating strategy into roadmaps.
- Hands-on judgement in distributed systems, production reliability, latency, observability, operational readiness, and code/design quality.
- Ownership of high-scale, production ML, AI, data, or infrastructure platforms.
- Product and stakeholder partnership across PM, research, science, operations, customers, or senior leadership.
- People development: mentoring, feedback, hiring, retention, growth, and raising the engineering bar.
- ML lifecycle depth: training data, model serving, evaluation, monitoring, feature/data pipelines, experimentation, and safe rollout.
- Ability to build reusable platform primitives and reduce operational toil.

## Positioning Implication

The CV should stop leading with "Staff IC who builds ML systems" and instead lead with:

> Engineering Manager for production ML and streaming data systems, with enough hands-on depth to guide architecture and enough operating maturity to lead teams, delivery, stakeholder alignment, and production quality.
