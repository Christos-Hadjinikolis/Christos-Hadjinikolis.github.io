## Role Summary Used

Anthropic's Connectivity team aims to make Claude "the most connected AI" by defining how agents access external systems safely, with the right permissions, and in ways enterprises can trust. The team ships for claude.ai, Claude Code, Cowork, and the API. It owns the MCP proxy that routes tool calls, OAuth and token management for authenticated connections, contributes to the MCP spec under the Linux Foundation, and maintains official Python and TypeScript SDKs.

The London Engineering Manager is expected to lead Connectivity engineers based in London while working closely with San Francisco engineering leadership and partner teams.

- Hire, coach, mentor, and develop engineers with an inclusive, equitable management approach.

- Partner with San Francisco connectivity leadership, Product, Security, and Platform to define roadmap and create clarity in ambiguity.
