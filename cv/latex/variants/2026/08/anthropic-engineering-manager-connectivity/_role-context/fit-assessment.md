# Fit Assessment

## Overall Read

This is a strong role to apply for, and it is a cleaner positioning match than a frontier-model or pure applied-ML-manager role.

The core argument is:

> Experienced Engineering Manager for production ML and backend/platform systems, with strong reliability instincts and hands-on GenAI harness work around tool routing, approvals, traces, replay, and auditable LLM workflows.

Anthropic Connectivity is not mainly asking for a model-training specialist. It is asking for an engineering manager who can lead infrastructure that lets AI systems access external tools safely, reliably, and with enterprise trust. That makes the user's blend of management, distributed systems, production ML, standards, developer tooling, and Promet/Skeleton work relevant.

## CV Tailoring Choices

- Repositioned the header from `Machine Learning Engineering Manager | Production ML & Applied AI Systems` to `Engineering Manager | AI Connectivity & Platform Reliability`.
- Rebuilt the sidebar around leadership, connectivity, platform, trust, and open-source rather than PyTorch/Hugging Face-heavy ML signalling.
- Kept profile links, dark header, side panel, subtle hyperlink styling, and no logo badges or boxed hyperlink borders.
- Rewrote the summary to foreground management, platform reliability, observable interfaces, evaluation/replay, rollback/fallback, incident response, standards/governance, and private GenAI tool-use work.
- Retargeted the Vortexa entry toward platform and reliability: distributed streaming estate, Kafka/Flink, rollback/fallback, shared on-call, incident response, observability/runbooks, and MTTR.
- Kept model/API serving and evaluation visible because Anthropic Connectivity still sits in AI product infrastructure and tool-call infrastructure, but moved it behind platform/reliability.
- Added explicit trust/interface/governance language: data lineage, schema management, Avro/interface contracts, SLAs, domain-owner proposals, and cross-team dependency clarity.
- Preserved people-management evidence: 6 direct reports, Product/SME/DS/DE span, 1:1s, performance/career development, hiring, coaching, delivery accountability, and operating cadence.
- Made Promet more relevant by framing it around AI connectivity infrastructure: local runtimes, MCP-style connector patterns, tool routing, schema validation, approval gates, durable state/memory, traces, streaming UX, deterministic replay/eval, and auditable LLM interactions.
- Added the public MCP/trust article as selected AI/connectivity work because it maps directly to Anthropic's tool-access and enterprise-trust problem space.
- Preserved dynamicio, skeleton-replay, and the JetBrains plugin as open-source/developer-tooling evidence.

## Application Strategy

Lead with management plus infrastructure reliability, not model credentials.

The referral/application thesis should be:

> I lead production AI/ML platform teams that make model-backed systems dependable, observable, recoverable, and governable. In parallel, I have been building the harness layer around LLMs: tool routing, approval gates, traces, and replay/evaluation. That gives me a concrete, hands-on view of the boundary Anthropic Connectivity is trying to harden.

For interviews, prepare four stories:

- Vortexa: running a production ML/data estate with on-call, rollback/fallback, observability, and delivery accountability.
- People management: 6 direct reports, 10-person cross-functional team, hiring/coaching/performance/career development.
- Stakeholder clarity: turning model-quality disputes and ambiguous semantics into shared definitions, SLAs/interfaces, and OKR-linked workstreams.
- Promet/Skeleton: private GenAI harness and public runtime-evidence tooling around tool-use, approval, traces, and replay.

## Remaining Gaps To Handle In Cover Letter Or Interview

- Say plainly that the strongest experience is adjacent to OAuth/token-management rather than direct ownership.
- Emphasize willingness to learn Anthropic-specific MCP/OAuth/token-management systems while contributing immediately on leadership, distributed systems, operational rigor, tool-use safety boundaries, standards, and enterprise trust.
- If asked about MCP, distinguish protocol familiarity, public writing, and Promet hands-on connector work from production MCP proxy ownership.
