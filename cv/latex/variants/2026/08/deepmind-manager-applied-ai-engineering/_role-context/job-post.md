## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 8 years of software development experience, including system design, data structures, and algorithms.
- 7 years of experience leading technical project strategy, ML design, and optimizing industry-scale ML infrastructure, for example model deployment, model evaluation, data processing, debugging, and fine tuning.
- 5 years of experience in a technical leadership role, overseeing projects, with 5 years of experience in a people management, supervision, or team leadership role.

## Preferred Qualifications

- 5 years of experience with one or more of: media generation, reinforcement learning, ML infrastructure, or specialization in another ML field.
- Experience with generative AI research or applications.
- Experience evaluating model performance, analyzing results, and implementing improvements.
- Experience with machine learning frameworks and libraries such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, etc.
- Experience in developing and shipping software products rapidly.
- Contributions to open-source projects.

## Role Summary

Lead development and deployment of novel applications leveraging Google's generative AI models. The role emphasizes rapid feature development, working across partner teams, translating research into real products, demonstrating latest-generation model capabilities, and building and shipping software while leading engineering teams. It calls for experience in early-stage or 0-to-1 product environments, product and business impact, relationships with research teams, reliability, software performance, mentoring, and architecture of new products and features.

## Responsibilities

- Lead a team in the design, development, and deployment of scalable generative AI applications and advocate industry best practices.
- Lead rapid development of new features, iterate based on evaluation results, and mentor the team toward a collaborative, high-performing environment.
- Collaborate with researchers and product managers to translate research advancements into tangible product features.
- Oversee software performance optimization and reliability of deployed applications.
- Lead architecture and development of new products and features from 0 to 1.
