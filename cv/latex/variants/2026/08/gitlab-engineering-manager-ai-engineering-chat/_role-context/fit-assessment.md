## Overall Positioning

This is a credible transferable fit, not an exact same-domain fit.

The strongest argument is that the candidate has led people and architecture around production AI/ML systems where correctness, evaluation, observability, latency, and operational trust mattered. GitLab Duo Chat is a newer product/domain shape, but the underlying management and engineering problems overlap: AI capability must become product software that teams can evaluate, debug, operate, and improve safely.

The CV therefore foregrounds Vortexa as the main evidence for people leadership, production ownership, technical judgement, reliability, model lifecycle, and delivery. GenAI/agentic familiarity is represented through Promet and public developer tooling, with restrained wording.
