Complete glossary · expand when a term is unfamiliar
Alpha
Return beyond a specified benchmark or risk model. The definition and implementation costs matter.
Backtest
A simulation using historical inputs and decision rules. It needs realistic availability, costs and execution assumptions.
IC · information coefficient
Correlation between a signal and later returns, often rank correlation. A measure of prediction, not automatically of tradable profit.
IR · information ratio
Conventionally, mean active return divided by the variability of active return, with an appropriate annualisation. Strategy research may use its own convention; check the methodology before comparing studies.
Signal decay
How predictive usefulness changes as information ages. The useful horizon depends on the task.
Aggregation / evidence window
The span of eligible past information included in a calculation. It can differ from the interval between updates.
Processing latency
Elapsed processing time after an input is available. End-to-end delay also includes source, queue, publication and delivery delays.
Publication cadence
How often outputs are released. A fast calculation can still be published infrequently.
Point-in-time data
Data representing what was available at a specified historical decision time, including the relevant versions and availability timestamps.
Look-ahead bias / leakage
A historical test uses information that would not have been available at the simulated decision, including later corrections or model knowledge.
Immutable snapshot
A saved state that is not changed after capture. It establishes that captured state, not every intermediate historical state.
Novelty / relevance
Whether information is new, and how directly it concerns an entity or topic. Both require definitions and evaluation.
Provenance / lineage
Records of where information came from and how it was transformed into an output.
Revision / vintage
A changed output, or the particular version available at a recorded time. Research must respect when changes became available.
Breadth
The number or range of eligible opportunities. A narrower evidence window can leave too few assets for a diversified strategy.
Systematic / discretionary
Systematic approaches use explicit decision rules; discretionary approaches rely on a person’s judgement. Real workflows may combine both.
Exposure / hedge
Sensitivity to a market move; a position intended to offset some of that risk.
Out-of-sample evaluation
Testing on observations not used to fit or select the model or rule. Repeated tuning on the test set can compromise it.
API / data contract
A programmatic interface / an agreement about schema, meaning, quality and change behaviour.
Go to market / renewal
How a product reaches and wins customers / a customer’s decision to continue a contract.
SLA
A defined service commitment. A recalled processing time or target is not itself an SLA.
