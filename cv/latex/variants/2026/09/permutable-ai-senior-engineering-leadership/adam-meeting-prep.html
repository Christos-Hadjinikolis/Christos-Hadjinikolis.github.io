Wait until ten minutes

Read before the meeting / 09

Choose the smallest useful change.

Streaming is one option. Diagnose the customer deadline and the measured critical path first.

Source delay+Queue / startup+Compute / I/O+Evidence wait+Client delivery
1 · Tune the current pipeline

Reuse warm workers, reduce repeated reads, improve matching and DAG dependencies — if those dominate.

2 · Shorter batches / a hybrid

Process expensive inference in batches; update selected signals more often. Preserve evidence windows.

3 · Stateful streaming where justified

Incremental state and lower scheduling delay; added responsibility for late data, recovery and revisions.

“What would we compare to know a faster path is better: time to first useful signal, revision rate, quality, cost and operational load?”

Test on a recorded news burst with duplicates and corrections. Compare equivalent evidence cutoffs; measure tail latency and customer-visible availability. Model batching efficiency, hot entities and backpressure may dominate.
