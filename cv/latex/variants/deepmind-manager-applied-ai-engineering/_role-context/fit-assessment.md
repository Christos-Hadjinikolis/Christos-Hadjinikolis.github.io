# Fit Assessment

## Overall Read

This is a credible targeted application, especially with a referral. The strongest argument is not "frontier model researcher", "PyTorch specialist", or someone whose current job is already applied GenAI product management. It is:

> I am an ML Engineering Manager and technical ML systems leader who leads teams through production ML, reliability, evaluation, model deployment, and the engineering operating model around model-backed products.

That matches much of the role's practical center: translating model capability into product behaviour, iterating through evaluation, leading the team that has to make it reliable, and bringing strong engineering discipline to AI product work. Promet, Skeleton Replay, and local-LLM prototype work are supporting evidence of applied AI interest and taste, not the primary identity claim.

## Application Strategy

Use the referral path. The CV is stronger when the profile stays honest: ML Engineering Manager, production ML infrastructure, evaluation/replay, reliability, and applied AI tooling interest. The story is not "I am already a DeepMind GenAI product manager"; it is "I lead teams that make model-backed systems dependable, observable, and useful under real production constraints."

For interviews, prepare three stories:

- Vortexa: leading a production ML/streaming estate and team at scale.
- Promet/Skeleton: building the GenAI harness and runtime-evidence layer while the repo stays private.
- Local-LLM prototype: a concrete 0-to-1 application story involving classification, entity resolution, query planning, grounded narrative generation, and provenance.
