# Matillion - Senior AI Engineering Manager, Maia Core

Source: user-pasted LinkedIn job description.
Captured: 2026-08-01.

## Role Summary

Matillion is hiring a Senior AI Engineering Manager for the Maia Core team, the cross-functional engineering group at the centre of Maia, Matillion's AI Data Automation platform.

The team develops and evolves the core services that power Maia. The role is an engineering management role, not an IC role, but technical credibility is described as non-negotiable. The manager is expected to understand the nuances of building and running agentic harnesses and to have first-hand experience with agentic AI tools in an engineering context.

Matillion frames engineering management around three disciplines:

- People
- Service
- Delivery

## People Expectations

- Lead a team spanning experienced engineers who need alignment and AI-native engineers who need craft development, ways of working, and growth.
- Build partnerships across Product, Design, Marketing, Customer Support, and Field Engineering.
- Inspire, motivate, and coach the team to do high-impact work.

## Service Expectations

- Own the core agentic harness that powers Maia and the services underpinning its capabilities.
- Lead incident management, observability, monitoring, alerting, and service health.
- Modernise service ownership with AI-powered approaches that reduce operational overhead and improve reliability and engineering efficiency.
- Help set the standard for service ownership across Matillion.

## Delivery Expectations

- Create the conditions for the team to deliver at pace without compromising quality.
- Optimise ways of working as Maia evolves rapidly.
- Make use of agentic tooling while keeping customers at the centre of decisions.
- Communicate delivery progress and service health to executive leadership.

## Candidate Signals Requested

- Agentic-first leadership experience.
- Hands-on familiarity with agentic tooling such as Claude Code or Codex.
- Strong SaaS and software engineering fundamentals.
- Comfort in Java and Python environments.
- Cloud-native SaaS systems, with focus on performance, reliability, and privacy.
- Stakeholder management across Product, Design, SRE, Security, and related functions.
- Comfort with ambiguity, rapid change, and incomplete information.

## Tailoring Boundary

This role is closer to the user's current direction than a pure data-platform role, but the CV must not imply:

- production ownership of an enterprise GenAI platform;
- production ownership of Matillion-style agentic harnesses;
- budget ownership;
- customer adoption for Promet;
- multiple-team or manager-of-managers scope;
- deep expert ownership of Java/Kotlin beyond defensible exposure.
