## Strongest Fit Argument

The strongest argument is not that the user already runs an enterprise GenAI platform. It is:

> The user has led production ML and streaming platform evolution at meaningful scale, including people leadership, architecture change, evaluation, deployment, observability, reliability, developer enablement, and stakeholder alignment. Promet adds current, hands-on agentic harness judgement without pretending to be production enterprise experience.

## Tailoring Choices

- Positioned the CV around `AI Harnesses & Production ML Platforms`.
- Put Promet in the summary and selected tooling section, but explicitly as private/local work.
- Reordered Vortexa bullets around People, Organisation Scaling, Service Ownership, Delivery/Stakeholders, Architectural Inflection Point, Production ML Platform, and Engineering Operating Model.
- Preserved the Vortexa metrics and team-scope boundaries.
- Kept maritime detail minimal and framed the estate as a client-facing ML/streaming backend platform.

## Claims Intentionally Not Made

- No claim of production Maia-like agentic SaaS ownership.
- No claim of enterprise users, budget ownership, customer adoption, or managed team for Promet.
- No claim of LiteLLM, BrainTrust, Temporal, or LLM gateway production ownership.
- No claim of managing multiple teams or being a manager of managers.
- No claim that the current Vortexa team operates agentically by default.
- No claim of deep Java/Kotlin expertise beyond defensible JVM/Kotlin exposure.
