# Interview Prep

## Five Stories To Prepare

1. **Kafka Streams to Flink as an architectural inflection point**
   - Explain why the migration mattered: independent state/checkpointing, operational UI, dataflow visibility, reduced partition-coupled scaling, and lower maintenance effort.
   - Be clear that the value was platform evolution and operational leverage, not a claimed throughput benchmark.

2. **Building Vortexa's production ML lifecycle**
   - Start from "the team did not have mature MLOps practices when I joined."
   - Cover MLflow, model/data versioning, service-based deployment, replay/evaluation interfaces, live accuracy monitoring, and explainability/auditability.
   - Tie it to Maia's need for trustworthy AI outputs without claiming LLM-specific production tooling.

3. **Promet as a private local AI harness shell**
   - Open with what it is: a local-first AI workbench/harness shell for memory-aware, voice-enabled, tool-using assistants.
   - Discuss orchestration, local runtimes, tool execution, prompt contracts, MCP-style connectors, approval/safety boundaries, traceability, replay, and UX.
   - Explicitly say it is private/personal and not enterprise production platform experience.

4. **People, service, and delivery at Vortexa**
   - Direct management of the MLE/DE/DS core and leadership of a 10-person cross-functional delivery team.
   - Hiring manager for 6 roles, 31+ candidates in that capacity, 60+ overall participation, full current team retention/progression.
   - Connect to service ownership: monitoring, runbooks, rollback/fallback, Jira alerts, shared on-call, MTTR under 30 min.

5. **Turning stakeholder friction into engineering work**
   - Use product-semantics and quality-dispute work: different stakeholders meant different things by the same domain concept.
   - Explain the managerial move: align definitions, turn ambiguity into measurable evaluation targets, then create RFC/workstream-ready interfaces.
   - Connect to Maia's need to keep platform roadmap shaped by product demand rather than platform preference.
