## Who Decides When AI Is Trustworthy?

**UCL · 7 October 2026**

The EU AI Act, Standards and the Limits of Assurance.

A discussion-led seminar connecting production machine learning, European AI standardisation and the evidence needed to approve an AI-assisted decision. A fictional home-robot exercise asks how your judgement changes when the task, permissions and consequences change.
